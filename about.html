PROJECT OVERVIEW

PhishGuard — Phishing Detection & Awareness

PhishGuard is an educational Flask application that demonstrates local rule-based message screening, URL structure analysis, scan history, PDF reporting and phishing awareness training.

What it can do

  • Identify configured warning phrases and suspicious message patterns.
  • Inspect URL structure for selected suspicious characteristics.
  • Record scan results in a local SQLite database.
  • Produce a PDF summary of recorded scans.
  • Teach safe response habits through a short quiz.

What it cannot do

  • It does not use an AI model or determine intent with certainty.
  • It does not query a live malware or domain reputation database.
  • It does not visit URLs, download attachments, or inspect email headers from a mailbox.
  • It can produce false positives and false negatives. A low score does not mean safe.
Privacy by design

Messages and URLs are processed by the local Flask application and stored in the local SQLite database. Do not scan sensitive private content on a shared computer. Do not deploy this development server publicly without authentication, HTTPS, CSRF protection, secure secrets and a privacy review.
